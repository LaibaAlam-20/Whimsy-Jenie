Hello, I'm Jenie!

I started baking at age 12 in my mother's kitchen, fascinated by how flour, butter and sugar could transform into something magical. What began as a hobby became a passion — and eventually, Whimsy Jenie.

After training in Paris and spending years perfecting recipes, I opened this bakery with one simple goal: to make every person feel special through the art of baking. Every cake, every cupcake, every macaron tells a story — and I'd love for it to be yours.
